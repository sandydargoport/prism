# Chores

Household jobs that come round again: empty the dishwasher, feed the pets, take the bins out. Each chore has a person (or anyone), how often it comes back, and how many points it earns. Points from approved chores fill the family's [goals](GOALS.md).

## Set it up

### What's in a chore

- **Title** (required) and an optional description.
- **Category**: Cleaning, Laundry, Dishes, Yard, Pets, Trash or Other. It sets the chore's icon.
- **Assigned to**: one family member, or nobody, which means anyone can do it.
- **Frequency**: daily, weekly, every two weeks, monthly, quarterly, every six months, yearly, or custom (every so many days).
- **Reset day** (optional): which day of the week a weekly chore comes back, which day of the month (1 to 28) for a monthly one, or the date (for example 03-15) for a yearly one.
- **Time of day** (optional): places the chore at that hour in the calendar's time views. Without one it sits at the top of the day.
- **Points**: what it earns when approved.
- **Requires approval**: on by default. [Ticking a chore off](#ticking-a-chore-off) explains what it does.
- **For** (optional): one of the household's pets, added under *Settings → Family Members*. A person still does the job; the chore shows the pet's name in its colour.

## Use it

### The Chores page

The page opens on what to do, as a list:

- **Due today**: everything due today, and anything overdue.
- **Tomorrow**
- **Later this week**

Each row shows the chore, a ring in the colour of the person it belongs to, and their name and points. Tap the ring to tick it. With nothing due this week, the page says so.

A chore for a pet says who did it today and when, for example *Emma 7:40 AM*. Once it is done for the day it stays at the foot of the list under **Pets, done today**, so everyone can see the dog was fed. Other chores leave the list once they are done.

A chore that is waiting for a parent to approve shows as one line, for example *Clean room: waiting for a parent to approve (done by Emma)*, with **Approve** for parents.

**Manage** (top right) shows the full list, where parents set chores up:

- **Add Chore**, and edit or delete a chore by tapping it.
- Group by **Person** or **None**, and sort by **Next Due**, **Frequency** or **Category**.
- Use **Hide Completed** and **Show Disabled** to choose which chores the list shows.
- Each chore has an on and off switch. A chore that is off keeps its history but stops coming back until it is on again.

Tap **Done** to go back to the do list.

### Ticking a chore off

**Requires approval** decides what happens when a child ticks a chore:

- **On** (the default): the tick waits for a parent. It shows as *waiting for a parent to approve*, and the points count once a parent taps **Approve**.
- **Off**: the tick counts at once, points included.

A parent's own tick always counts at once.

When the chore is done, its next due date moves on by its frequency (or to its reset day), and it drops off the do list until then.

#### On the wall, without the PIN

*Settings → Security → Require the PIN for every change* decides whether the wall asks for a PIN to tick a chore.

- **On**: every tick asks who you are and for your PIN, as it always has.
- **Off** (the default on new installs): anyone at the wall can tick a chore without a PIN. The tick goes to the person it is assigned to. A chore for anyone asks *Who did it?* and offers the family to choose from. **Undo** appears for a short while in case of a wrong tap.

Ticks made this way are written to the activity log. The wall can never approve a chore: on one that requires approval, its tick waits for a parent like any other. Approving, editing and settings always need a parent.

### On the dashboard

On the Glance front page, a quiet line in the bottom corner says how many chores are left today, with a ring per chore in its person's colour. Tap a ring to tick that chore.

The Chores widget on a grid dashboard lists the same chores.

### Points and goals

Approved chores add their points to the person who did them, and those points fill that person's goals in priority order. [Goals & Points](GOALS.md) covers how.

## Details

### Tips

- Keep chores small and specific: *Empty the dishwasher* is easier to tick than *Kitchen*.
- Leave **Requires approval** on for chores you want to check, and turn it off for the small daily ones.
- Use **Assigned to: nobody** for jobs anyone can grab; the wall asks who did it.
