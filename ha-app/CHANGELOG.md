# Prism add-on changelog

The add-on ships the same Prism release as the standalone Docker image, so these
notes are the release notes for the whole app. Earlier releases are on the
[full changelog](https://sandydargoport.github.io/prism/CHANGELOG/).

## [1.31.0] – 2026-11-25

Ships 24 database migrations, applied automatically on start.

### What moved where
- **The side rail and the top bar** became the dock: one labelled row of pages along the bottom of every page, on landscape, portrait and phone screens. On a wall dashboard with **Auto-hide navigation** on, the dock is hidden behind a small **Menu** tab on the bottom edge and a tap anywhere brings it back; with it off, the dock stays on screen, as the rail and bar did.
- **The top bar's screen controls** (Edit layout, Reload, Performance, Babysitter mode, Away mode, and the button that started the old screensaver, now **Idle now**) are in the dock's **More**, under **This screen**, each with its name, on every page. From a page other than the dashboard, **Edit layout** opens the dashboard with the editor open.
- **The More panel** has three groups of the same tiles: **This screen**, **Pages** (the pages that are not on the dock) and **Prism** (Settings, Help, sign in). On a phone, This screen comes last. Which pages sit on the dock is chosen in *Settings → Features*.
- **Sign in on a phone** is in **More**.
- **The front page** is the built-in **Glance** dashboard on a new install; an existing install keeps its own. Glance leads with today as a list; the day as an arc is an option in its **Widgets** sheet (**Show the day as an arc**).
- **Chores**: the filters, on and off switches, points and categories are under **Manage**. On a phone, History is in the page's menu.
- **Shopping**: the categories are under **Manage**, one row each. The progress bar is in **Shopping mode**, and the page's header shows how many items are checked off. The pencil at the end of an item's row edits it. Checked items are under **Checked off**. On a phone, Scan Barcode, Send to the store, Shopping mode and New list are in the page's menu.
- **Manage calendars** on the Calendar page is in the view menu.
- **On a phone, the round button** at the bottom right is each page's Add, and only that. A phone's cards are under **More → This phone**. Meals and Recipes keep their import and sync choices behind **More ways to add** (the "..." button) in the header.
- **Babysitter and Away modes** are named **Babysitter mode** and **Away mode** in **More → This screen**, apart from the Babysitter page.
- **Appearance** is in three groups: **Look** (colour scheme, palette, seasonal theme), **Background and away** (wallpaper, Away mode, night sky) and **All screens** (units, language).
- **A phone never idles**; it dims and locks itself.
- **The layout editor** is a canvas on every device: the dashboard at the size of the screen that shows it, with three tabs beside it, **Screen**, **Widgets** and **Look** (see Added). The photo frame's arrangement is reached from **Screen > When idle > Arrange the photo frame**.
- **One screen's own settings** (when it goes idle, what it shows idle and at night, how much darker at night, orientation, performance, the on-screen keyboard, auto-hide) are in that screen's editor, on the **Screen** tab, instead of *Settings → Appearance* and *Settings → Input*.
- **Settings → Displays** is *Settings → Screens*.
- **The Calendar view** is gone; a dashboard set to it is a regular dashboard with the same widgets.

### Added
- **The screen owns its day.** What a screen shows idle by day and at night, when night is, and how much darker at night are the screen's own, set in its editor under **Screen > When idle** and saved to the screen. A switch to another dashboard changes what the screen shows, not how it idles. A device with no name uses the household's starting day until it sets its own, and a screen named on it keeps that day. A new install starts with the dashboard, quieter, from dusk to dawn, 50% darker at night. On upgrade each named screen takes how its normal dashboard idled and keeps its darkness; a screen whose switch went to a dashboard that idled differently now idles as its normal dashboard during the switch too, and the upgrade log names it. Choosing the dashboard as the night look no longer turns on the darkness by itself. ([#1345](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1345))
- **Feedback in More.** The bottom of the More panel asks one question at a time, with a one-tap answer per install that can be changed later. The first asks where the page buttons go: in the bar along the bottom, or in a sidebar. The answer goes through the anonymous install counter, so with anonymous stats off (the Home Assistant add-on's **Anonymous stats** option, or More > Settings > About) nothing is sent and the card says so. ([#1311](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1311))
- **One editor everywhere: the canvas.** On any device, **Edit** opens the dashboard laid out at the size of the screen that shows it (1920 × 1080 when none has reported a size), scaled to fit the window. On a laptop the editing panels sit in a column beside it, with one always open; on a phone they are below it. No panel covers the canvas or moves it. **Shown at** picks another size: a named screen or a common size. Editing another screen from *Settings → Screens* uses the same canvas, now up to the window's full height instead of 45% of it. A named screen's **Edit** opens its own editor on the same canvas, at its own size, and **Done** brings its settings home, so the editor looks the same on the wall, a laptop and a phone. ([#1265](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1265), [#1280](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1280))
- **Ingredients and steps edited in Prism go back to Tandoor and Mealie too, keeping their structure.** A line you did not change keeps the other app's ingredient with its food, unit and amount; a new or edited line goes in as a plain note, and a removed one is removed. The steps' text goes back to a recipe with one step; one with several keeps its steps. The review says how many lines go as notes and whether the steps stay. ([#1215](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1215))
- **Recipe edits made in Prism go back to Tandoor and Mealie, after review.** On a server whose token can write, the recipe sync lists recipes edited in Prism since the last sync and sends their name, description, servings and times once ticked. A recipe changed in both places goes to the newer side, and the review says which. A recipe deleted in Prism is offered unticked and stays in the other app unless ticked. ([#1207](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1207))
- **Meal-plan changes made in Prism go back to Tandoor and Mealie, after review.** On a server whose token can write, the meal-plan sync also lists what changed in Prism since the last sync: meals added (from today on), moved or renamed, and removed. Each is ticked or unticked before anything is sent; meals planned before the last sync and every removal start unticked, and removing many at once is held back. A meal's time of day is no longer compared, so a time set in Prism is not pulled back over. ([#1206](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1206))
- **A Tandoor or Mealie server says whether its token can write.** In the recipe and meal-plan sync, each connected server's card says **Reads only** or **Reads and writes**, checked without changing anything on the server. A Tandoor token with the `read write` scope can write; a `read` token reads only and keeps working as before, with a line on how to make one that writes. A Mealie token always can. A server connected earlier is checked the next time the sync opens. ([#1205](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1205))
- **Edit any screen from a phone.** *Settings → Screens* (was *Displays*) lists each named screen with whether it is on and one **Edit**. On another device, Edit opens that screen's editor over a live picture of it, at the screen's own size and shape, with the same tabs under it at the phone's size. The screen shows a small "Being edited from a phone" note while the editor is open, and shows the result a few seconds after **Done**. Nothing changes on the phone itself. Each row's other buttons are gone; what they set is in the editor's **Screen** tab. A screen reports its size with its heartbeat. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#1153](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1153))
- **The Screen tab holds the screen's whole day.** More > Edit layout > Screen starts with one sentence that says what the screen does, for example "Hallway shows Main. On school days it shows Bus board around bus time and stays awake for it. Idle for 2 minutes, it becomes a photo frame. At night the photo frame is 50% darker." It follows each change before **Done**, and says so when something will not happen ("Never idles, so it has no night look"). Under it, a strip of today shades the screen's night, draws each switch as a band in the colour of its row's swatch, and marks now. Then the rows: **Shows** (**Normally**, the dashboard it shows outside its times, then **Add a time** and **Add bus time**), **When idle** (**Idle after**, what it shows **By day** and **At night**, **Darker** at night, **Arrange the photo frame**, and under **Advanced** when night is and the photo frame's motion), **This screen** (light or dark, photos behind the dashboard) and **Advanced** (orientation, performance, on-screen keyboard, auto-hide, sign out after, notices). A line says that a switch changes what the screen shows and night changes how it idles. ([#1152](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1152), [#1170](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1170), [#1281](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1281), [#1294](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1294), [#1349](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1349))
- **Glance is the one built-in dashboard, and it can be edited.** Glance stays the live scene while editing, and each change in its **Widgets** sheet shows at once. The **Dashboard** sheet lists it first with a **Built in** mark, **Reset to default** and **Make a copy**; it cannot be deleted, in the app or through the API. A new install starts with Glance only and can make dashboards of its own, and setup's last step shows Glance and says where editing is. The **Calendar** ready-made dashboard, the *Front page: Simple / Custom* card in *Settings → Appearance* and the one-time Simple offer on the wall are gone. ([#1151](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1151))
- **Subtasks from Google Tasks and steps from Microsoft To Do.** A Google Tasks subtask, or a To Do step, now comes into Prism under its task, indented on the To-dos page and in the Tasks widget; both used to be skipped. A Google subtask keeps its own due date and notes, in Google's order. Ticking a subtask or a step off in Prism ticks it off at the provider, and ticking the task leaves its subtasks as they are. If a task and its subtasks are deleted in Google, only the task is listed for review, and confirming it removes the subtasks too. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#1200](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1200), [#1201](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1201), [#1202](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1202), [#324](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/324))
- **Passcodes as well as PINs.** In *Settings → Family Members*, a member can sign in with a **Passcode** of 6, 8, 10 or 12 lowercase letters and numbers instead of a PIN. On the pad, a member with a passcode gets **ABC**, which shows the letters, and **123** to go back; the card widens while the letters show, and letters typed on a keyboard work too. Existing members keep their PINs, the attempt limit and lockout are the same, and a child who cannot spell yet is better on a PIN. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#1197](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1197), [#347](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/347))
- **Chores for a pet, and who did them today.** Pets are listed under *Settings → Family Members* with a name, a kind (cat, dog or other) and a colour; a pet has no account, PIN or points. A chore or reminder can be **For** a pet, and shows the pet's name in its colour on the Chores page and in the Chores widget. Each one says who did it today and when ("Emma 7:40 AM, Jordan 5:15 PM"), and once it is done for the day it stays on the Chores page under **Pets, done today**, so nobody feeds the dog twice. Removing a pet keeps its chores. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#996](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/996), [#856](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/856))
- **Hide in Prism without signing in first.** The event sheet shows **Hide in Prism** to anyone not signed in as a child; the tap asks for a parent's PIN, then hides the event. ([#1048](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1048))
- **Add a category from Shopping's Manage.** Below the category rows, **Add category** creates one and, on a list that shows chosen categories, adds it to that list at once. It used to take *Manage Categories* in the menu and then ticking it in *Edit List*. ([#1053](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1053))
- **Glance: the front page as one scene.** A built-in dashboard, **Glance**, draws the front page as one scene over the photo instead of a grid of cards: the date and clock top left, the weather as one line ("Partly cloudy, high 79") top right, **Next** (the event under way or about to start, in its person's colour, with its time) and **Later today** across the middle, the coming days as sentences bottom left, and quiet lines bottom right. Next and each later row take a tap only on their words, so a tap to wake the screen does not open an event. The coming days are a hanging day column ("Tomorrow  Soccer at 10."): two things a day, three days ahead, built from templates in English and German with no outside service, naming the person a calendar belongs to. The quiet lines say how many chores are left today, with a ring in each person's colour that ticks the chore, the shopping list's count with a plus to add, and, when a birthday is two to seven days away, a line offering a task to mail a card (**Add it** or **Not now**, quiet from 8 pm). A line with nothing to say drops out. In portrait the clock and weather sit on top, the day across the upper middle, the coming days below it and the quiet lines at the foot. A new install starts on it. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#907](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/907), [#805](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/805), [#911](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/911), [#934](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/934), [#1030](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1030), [#993](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/993), [#1034](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1034))
- **The day as an arc, as an option.** **Show the day as an arc** in Glance's **Widgets** sheet (off by default) draws the day as the sun's path in place of the list: 6 am to 10 pm (earlier or later when an event needs it), hours already spent squeezed to a quarter of the width, the height following the real sun from sunrise and sunset, never flat, with a smaller curve below the horizon at night. Each timed event is a bead in its person's colour, labelled "Soccer 4" or "Soccer until 5:30"; on a busy day labels are placed where they fit, with a hairline to their bead, and an event without a label stays a bead that opens with a tap. Between moonrise and moonset the moon's own fainter, dotted curve is drawn, and at night the moon sits on it in its phase. "Nothing else today" when the day is done. ([#909](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/909), [#910](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/910), [#994](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/994), [#993](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/993))
- **Glance goes quieter when idle and warm at night.** When the wall goes idle, the sentences and quiet lines fade, only Next stays (or, with the arc, only the next event is labelled), and the clock and weather stay where they are. The first tap only wakes the screen. During the screen's night, set in **Screen > When idle > Advanced** (by default half an hour after sunset to half an hour before sunrise, 8 pm to 6 am without sun times), the ink turns warm, the photo is veiled and the moon shows in its phase. ([#926](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/926))
- **Glance's parts in the layout editor.** Editing Glance shows its widgets in the editor's **Widgets** panel instead of the grid: for the clock and date, weather, the day and the coming days, the birthday nudge, chores left and the shopping list, **Show** and **Stays when idle**; for the scene, how many days ahead the sentences cover (1 to 7) and text size (Normal, Large, Larger). Shared layouts carry these settings. ([#927](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/927))
- **Glance on a phone.** On a phone, Glance shows a phone page instead of widget cards: the time with the temperature and the date with the condition in two aligned rows, **Next** in its own card, then later today, the coming days and the chores and grocery lines. It is one screen tall, without rubber-band bounce. A grid layout keeps its cards. ([#928](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/928), [#1030](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1030), [#1034](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1034))
- **One labelled dock on every page.** The dock is one row of pages (Home, Calendar, Chores, Shopping, Meals and Photos by default), **More** and Sign in (or the signed-in person), each with its label and the current page marked. On a wall dashboard whose screen has **Auto-hide navigation** on (in the editor's **Screen > Advanced**), it sits as a soft **Menu** tab on the bottom edge, which shrinks to a faint nub after 8 quiet seconds; a touch brings the dock up over the page, so nothing moves, and it goes after ten quiet seconds. With it off, the dashboard keeps the dock on screen and ends above it. A screen that never chose follows the household: off on an upgraded install, on for a new one with the park scenery. Every other page keeps it on screen at the bottom, with room left so the last row of a list scrolls clear; on a phone it holds five pages and More. **More** has Settings and Help at the top, the other pages under **Pages**, and the screen's controls under **This screen**, each as an icon above its name, five a row. *Settings → Features* chooses which pages sit on the dock (Home and up to five ticked pages, exactly those), and the dock follows a change at once. A page switched off holds no place there; switched back on, it returns to the dock, or to More if the dock is full by then. **Home** goes back to the dashboard the screen came from. ([#922](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/922), [#923](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/923), [#924](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/924), [#925](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/925), [#1006](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1006), [#1014](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1014), [#989](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/989), [#1172](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1172), [#1173](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1173), [#1232](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1232))
- **One event sheet, opened by tapping an event anywhere.** Tapping an event on Glance, on the dashboard's calendar widget (where it did nothing before) or on the Calendar page opens the same sheet: the person's colour dot, the title, the event as a sentence ("Thursday, November 5, 4 to 5:30"), its notes, **Hide in Prism** (parents), **Delete** and **Edit**. A **+** on the calendar widget adds an event on the day it shows. ([#836](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/836))
- **Ticks without the PIN, a household choice.** *Settings → Security → Require the PIN for every change* is on for existing installs and off for new ones. Off, a signed-out wall can tick a chore, a task or a shopping item and add a shopping item in one tap, with "Ticked. Undo"; a chore with nobody assigned asks who did it. A wall tick never approves, so a chore that needs approval waits for a parent. Editing, deleting, approving and settings still need a parent. Wall changes are limited to 60 a minute for the household and written to the activity log. ([#835](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/835), [#809](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/809))
- **Photos crossfade and slowly zoom, keeping the subject in view.** The photos behind the dashboard and the photo frame share one photo stage: the next photo is decoded first, then fades in over the current one for 2.2 seconds, so the picture never dips to black. Each photo zooms slowly in or out, alternating, anchored on its subject, and is cropped so the subject sits where no widget or Glance text covers it. Prism finds each photo's subject on the wall itself, a few photos every few minutes, and no photo leaves the install. The zoom is off under reduced motion, below the full performance level, for a pinned photo and when rotation is set to never; a screen whose photo changes keep dropping frames steps down a performance level. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#954](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/954), [#955](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/955), [#956](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/956), [#957](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/957), [#802](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/802))
- **When and where a photo was taken.** The photos behind the dashboard and the photo frame say when the photo on screen was taken ("March", "March last year", "March 2019", "On this day, 2021") in a bottom corner, lifted clear of anything small already there, and hidden when neither corner has room or on a phone. In *Settings → Photos → Captions and On this day*, **Show when photos were taken** is on by default and **Show where photos were taken** is off; turned on, it adds the photo's town, looked up once per photo from OpenStreetMap using a location rounded to about a kilometre, as the setting says. Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#958](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/958), [#992](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/992), [#1034](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1034), [#1351](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1351))
- **An On this day mix.** **On this day**, in the wallpaper card in *Settings → Appearance* (off by default), mixes photos taken on today's date in earlier years into the rotation, every other turn, on the screen's own date. ([#959](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/959))
- **A leave-by line for the bus.** *Settings → Bus tracking*, on a route, has **Minutes to walk to the stop** (0 to 60). With a walk time, Now & Next shows "Leave · Morning bus · in 6 min" above the bus line, from the predicted arrival (or the timetable without a live prediction), amber in the last five minutes, then "now". Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#900](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/900))
- **Display rules for each calendar.** In *Calendar → View → Manage calendars*, each calendar has three rules: **Show its events only as "Busy"** (title, notes and place never reach the screen), **Birthdays as name and age** (a birthday event reads as the name and age when Prism's birthday list has that name with a birth year), and **Hide today's events after a time** (for example, bin day after 11:00, back the next day). Ships a database migration, applied automatically on start. ([#877](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/877))
- **Keys in the layout editor.** With a widget selected, **Delete** or **Backspace** removes it, with Undo, and the arrow keys move it one cell, or resize it in resize mode. No key acts while typing in a field or with Ctrl, Alt or Cmd held. ([#987](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/987))
- **Align for the clock and weather.** In the layout editor's text panel for the clock and the weather, **Across** (Auto, Left, Centre, Right) and **Up/down** (Auto, Top, Middle, Bottom) set where the contents sit. On Auto, a small tile (a third of the width or less) touching a screen corner hugs that corner, and any other tile is centred. A small weather tile shows the icon, temperature and condition on one line each, and the rest once the tile is wide enough. ([#905](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/905), [#988](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/988))
- **Long event titles slide.** In the month, week, week-card and day views, a title too long for its event rests, slides left to its end, rests, fades and starts again, three at a time. It never moves right while it can be seen. Nothing slides under reduced motion. ([#901](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/901))
- **Keep this device signed in, and a list of signed-in devices.** *Settings → Security → Signed-in devices* lets a parent keep the device in hand signed in: its session lasts 30 days from last use (it was 7) and up to a year from sign-in (it was 30 days). The card lists the parent's other signed-in devices by name ("iPhone · Safari") with sign-in and last-used dates, with **Sign out** on each and **Sign out all other devices**. ([#1044](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1044))
- **A one-time What's new note.** After the update, each screen shows once what moved and where, with **Got it**. It does not show on a new install, and the same list is the first section of Help. ([#1032](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/1032))
- **A Chores page in the user guide.** [Chores](features/CHORES.md) covers the do list and Manage, what a chore holds, how a tick counts with and without approval, ticking on the wall without the PIN, and how points reach goals. ([#842](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/842))
- **Events are a tint with a colour bar.** In the agenda, the vertical week list, and the all-day and multi-day bars of the week and month views, an event is now a pale tint of its person's colour with a solid bar of that colour on its leading edge, in the normal text colour, instead of white text on a solid block. It reads in light and dark for every member colour. The **Cards** display mode is unchanged. ([#801](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/801))
- **Calendars and a look in setup.** The setup wizard has two new steps before Done. **Calendars** takes a calendar link (Google's secret iCal address, an iCloud public calendar, an Outlook published calendar, a school or team feed), as many as wanted, or can be skipped. **Look** offers **Photo** (park scenery behind glass cards), **Night** (the same, dark on every screen that has not chosen light or dark) and **Paper** (plain warm cards, no photo), applied when setup finishes; running setup again skips it. A new **Paper** palette joins *Settings → Appearance → Palette*, and new installs start on it. The calendar's **Connect a calendar** button now opens the calendar list, where a link can be added. ([#808](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/808))
- **Park scenery and glass cards on a new install.** A new install shows six US National Park Service landscapes behind the dashboard, in rotation, until the household adds photos of its own, and its first cards are glass: the theme's card colour at 65%, see-through and blurred, light in light mode and dark in dark mode. When the wall is idle the calendar fades, leaving the clock and weather over the photo. **Glass** is also a new choice in the layout editor's **Fill** menu, beside **Frosted**, and the **Ambient** templates use it. Performance Mode drops the blur and thickens the glass. Installs that already finished setup keep their background and their auto-hide setting. ([#808](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/808))
- **A new install invites a calendar.** Finishing setup for the first time hides Wishes, Travel and Weekend (*Settings → Features* shows them again). Until a calendar is connected and while nothing is on it, the calendar invites one, with a **Connect a calendar** button. Installs that already finished setup, and running setup again, are not changed. ([#808](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/808))
- **Custom repeats for events.** The event form's repeat menu has a **Custom…** choice: every so many days, weeks, months or years; on chosen days of the week; monthly or yearly on the date or on, say, the third Wednesday; and ending never, on a date, or after a number of times. ## [1.28.0] – 2026-10-01

Dates and times now follow the household's time zone wherever Prism decides what day or hour it is, instead of whichever zone the server or the viewing device happens to run in. Ships three database migrations, applied automatically on start: task due dates move to a date plus an optional time, older iCal and CalDAV all-day events are converted to the form the calendar reads, and timestamps a non-UTC database wrote in its own zone are converted to UTC. The last one only changes anything where the database's default zone is not UTC, as on some Home Assistant installs.

### Changed
- **A task's due is a date and an optional time.** It used to be a single timestamp that each form and each sync provider wrote differently, so a task could move a day depending on who wrote it and where it was read. The API now takes `dueDate` as `YYYY-MM-DD` and `dueTime` as `HH:mm`; an ISO date-time from an older client is still accepted and read in the household zone. Existing tasks are converted on upgrade.

### Fixed
- **Only one keyboard appears on a touchscreen tablet.** When Prism's on-screen keyboard opened for a field, nothing told the browser the page had its own, so on Android and Windows tablets the system keyboard opened as well. While Prism's keyboard serves a field, the field now asks the browser not to open one, and gets its original setting back when it lets go. Phones and mouse use are unaffected. Thanks @yeojwal for the report. ([#498](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/498))
- **The field you are typing in stays above the on-screen keyboard.** The page assumed the keyboard was shorter than it is, scrolled only the nearest scrolling area, and did nothing for pop-up forms or for a field in the last row of a full-height page. The keyboard's height now comes from one place, every scrolling area between the field and the page moves as needed, and a pop-up form such as Add Item moves into the space above the keys while the keyboard is open. Thanks @yeojwal for the report. ([#499](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/499))
- **All-day events from iCal and CalDAV calendars cover the right days on a server outside UTC.** Date-only events were stored at midnight in the server's time zone, while the calendar reads all-day dates at UTC midnight, so west of UTC a one-day event also covered the next day, and east of UTC it started a day early. They are now stored the way Google all-day events already were, and existing events correct themselves on the next sync. CalDAV all-day events were also being stored as timed midnight-to-midnight events, because the check for a date-only value never matched; they now show as all-day. Thanks @flightlinefoundation for the diagnosis. ([#518](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/518))
- **A fresh Docker install's first dashboard fits the screen.** The database seed created the default dashboard in the old four-column grid units, which load as a tall portrait layout on the current grid, so on a landscape screen it was letterboxed, overflowed, and lost rows at both ends. The seed now uses the standard default layout in current units. Existing layouts are untouched. Thanks @flightlinefoundation for the measurements. ([#519](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/519))
- **A list on the Shopping page can be scrolled past again on a touchscreen.** Every list card told the browser not to scroll for a gesture that began on it, and a finger landing anywhere on a card started a reorder, so on a display where the lists cover the width there was no background left to push: trying to scroll the page picked a list up and moved it. The card header, which is the part carrying the grip, is now the handle, and the rest of the card scrolls like any other part of the page. Thanks @yeojwal for the report. ([#501](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/501))
- **The German calendar's schedule view is now labelled "Terminplan".** "Zeitplan" reads as a generic timetable rather than a list of appointments. Thanks to @Buanz.
- **The PIN pad can no longer sign a person in as somebody else.** The login pad is served the family as a numbered list rather than as user ids, and the server turns the number it is given back into a member by asking for that list again. The list was ordered by display position and creation time, neither of which is unique: members written in one statement, which is what a seed or a restore does, share both, and the database is free to return rows that tie in any order it likes. The two lists could then disagree, so the pad could show one member, send their position, and have the server resolve it to a different member and accept that member's PIN. The ordering now ends with the member's own id, which nothing else shares, so there is no tie left to resolve and both ends always see the same list. ([#484](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/484))
- **Weekday column headers are named correctly at UTC+13 and UTC+14.** The names were taken from a date built at noon UTC, which is already the next day in Samoa, Tonga and Kiribati, so every header there read one day late. They are now taken from local noon.
- **The setup wizard's household choices are saved.** Before any parent account has signed in, the server refused every setting except the PIN length, and the wizard did not check the answer. The time zone, week start and location chosen on the Household step were lost while the screen showed them as set, and switching off the anonymous update check on the last step did not take effect. These settings are now accepted during setup and checked for a valid value, the Household step saves the time zone and week start it shows even when they are left unchanged, and the update-check switch reverts if its change was not saved. Installs that never stored a household time zone save one the next time a parent signs in or opens Prism, taken from that device; a device reporting UTC is skipped, and a stored zone is never replaced.
- **Timestamps no longer read hours off on a database set to a local zone.** Every database session now runs in UTC, so a message posted a moment ago no longer shows as hours old. Rows written in the database's own zone before this are converted once on upgrade. Thanks @flightlinefoundation for spotting it. ([#526](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/526))
- **Chores, points and birthdays count days in the household zone.** On a default Docker install the server's clock is UTC, so a daily chore done in the evening in the Americas could skip a day, tomorrow's chores appeared the evening before, and points for a new week or month started arriving hours early. Scheduling, the due list, overdue colours, point periods, goal periods and upcoming birthdays all follow the household's calendar date now. Thanks @flightlinefoundation for the birthday report. ([#521](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/521))
- **Voice answers use the household's day and clock.** The voice calendar, chores, tasks, meals, bus and messages routes asked about the server's "today" and spoke times in its zone, so a 9 AM event could be read out as 2 PM. They use the household zone now, and all-day events are dated by their own date. ([#531](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/531))
- **All-day events and drags land on the right day.** Dragging an all-day event west of UTC dropped it a day late, the event detail labelled it with the day before, and the dashboard card dropped it in the evening or kept it into the next morning. All-day events are now read by their stored date, and a timed event keeps its wall-clock start when moved, across a DST change too. The calendar also moves to the new day at midnight on a display that is never reloaded.
- **Older iCal and CalDAV all-day events show on the right day.** Events stored before the earlier all-day fix, and no longer revisited by sync, are converted on upgrade.
- **Synced calendar times and ids no longer depend on the server's zone.** CalDAV times without a known zone are read in the household zone, recurring all-day events keep a stable id (existing ones carry over without being flagged for deletion), and all-day events sent to Google are dated correctly.
- **"Today" follows the display zone, not the device's.** On a device in a different zone from the one the display follows, the Today label, past-day shading, overdue colours, the meals page, the seasonal theme and the last-synced times could land on the wrong day for part of every day.
- **Weather is dated in the weather location's zone.** Pirate Weather and OpenWeather days and their morning, afternoon and evening periods followed the server's clock, the calendar placed each day's forecast a column early west of UTC, and moonrise, moonset and the sun arc could show the wrong day.
- **Bus routes are tracked on the household clock.** A route's scheduled time was placed on the server's clock, so its tracking window could miss the bus and it could be reported overdue hours early. Trip dates and weekdays are household ones now, and a Sunday route shows as active in the voice status.
- **A visit on 1 January is filed under its own year** in travel pins and trips, instead of the year before west of UTC.
- **The same photo in OneDrive and Immich shows once.** Immich capture times are now stored on the camera clock, the way OneDrive reports them, so the duplicate check matches them. Existing Immich photos are corrected on the next sync.
- **Backup times show in the display zone** and in the chosen 12- or 24-hour format.
- **Time labels and grid heights are right on DST change days.** Labels read the display zone's wall clock directly, and week and day views size an event by wall-clock time, so a night with a clock change no longer draws an extra or missing hour. The 12- or 24-hour setting also applies from the first paint, and message ages, tasks and chores refresh on their own.
- **Task and chore due days follow the display zone.** Overdue marks and the Today, Tomorrow and "in N days" labels on task and chore cards went by the device's clock, so on a device in a different zone from the one the display follows they could be a day off for part of every day.
- **CalDAV recurring events that started long ago sync again.** A repeating event whose series began years (or, for a daily event, months) before the sync range now shows its current occurrences, and a daily event is no longer cut off after 100 days. EXDATE, UNTIL and COUNT still apply, and synced ids do not change. ([#533](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/533))
- **Chores completed by voice follow the same approval rules as the app.** A parent's own chore completed by voice is approved and counted at once. A child's waits for a parent to approve it, as it does in the app, and now keeps its points so approving it counts them. Chores that require approval always wait for approval in the app. ([#530](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/530))
- **MCP tools send what their API expects.** `list_meals` filters by its dates, `create_meal` saves to the right day instead of failing, and `create_maintenance_item` keeps the due date and sends the category and schedule the API requires. Completing a chore, posting a message and creating a goal no longer fail on renamed fields, and updating a chore, event or shopping item uses the method the API accepts. ([#532](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/532))
- **MCP tools read times in the household time zone.** A time such as `2026-10-04T15:00` is placed in the household zone, times with an offset are also accepted, and all-day events take plain dates. A new `get_household_time` tool reports the zone and today's date, and events come back with local start and end times. The zone comes from a new `/api/household-time` endpoint, which other API clients can use too.
- **Voice, Home Assistant and MCP work with the authentication wall on.** The wall only accepted a signed-in browser or a trusted device, so every API token caller was turned away while it was switched on. A valid API token now gets through; each route still checks what the token is allowed to do.
- **Hidden action buttons show on touchscreens again.** Since the 1.27.0 styling update, buttons revealed on hover (mark cooked, edit and delete on the meal plan, Undo on the Meals widget, and the chore, message and gift idea actions) stayed invisible on touchscreens wider than a phone. They now show dimmed on touch devices; hovering with a mouse works as before. Thanks @yeojwal for the report. ([#523](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/523))
- **The travel globe fits on portrait screens.** It opened at a fixed size that was wider than a narrow portrait screen, so its sides were cut off, and short landscape windows lost the top and bottom. It now shrinks to fit when needed, including after the screen is rotated, and screens where it already fitted look the same. Thanks @yeojwal for the report. ([#524](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/524))
- **Performance mode removes the cards' blur again.** Since 1.27.0 the cards and the screensaver kept their background blur with performance mode on. Thanks to @sanko-oz.
- **The first Add dialog no longer blanks the dashboard.** Opening Add task, chore, message or shopping item for the first time could clear the dashboard for a moment while the dialog loaded. Thanks to @sanko-oz.
- **Reduced motion no longer animates every element when a dialog opens.** Thanks to @sanko-oz.

## [1.27.0] – 2026-09-19

### Changed
- **Widgets no longer refetch on every screensaver cycle, and stop polling while the screensaver covers them.** Mounting a widget now reads the last value and the time it was fetched, and goes to the network only if that value is older than the widget's own refresh interval, so the copies of the widgets the screensaver draws come up with data instead of loading from cold every time the display goes idle. Polling pauses while the screensaver is up and does one catch-up refresh when the display is woken, rather than one per tick that was missed. The screensaver's own widgets keep polling, because they are the ones on screen. Away Mode and Babysitter Mode keep polling too, since either can be switched on from another device and decides what the display shows. Several live copies of the same endpoint now share one poll between them instead of each running its own timer. Measured on the demo instance: 6.5 minutes of screensaver went from 48 requests to 16, and the screensaver appearing went from 4 requests to 2, with no loading placeholders. ([#336](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/336))

### Under the hood
- Checks that could only ever pass in one environment no longer ship here. Two required checks were retired along with the workflows behind them, and one, "Repo hygiene", replaces them, so a contributor's pull request is now gated only by checks their own checkout can run. What stays is what a fork benefits from: the secret-shape scan, which fails on a committed API key or private-key block in any checkout, and the rule that screenshots under `docs/demos/` may only arrive from the workflow that generates them against a seeded database. A checkout can add its own commit-time checks: `.husky/pre-commit`, `commit-msg` and `pre-push` run `.husky/local/<hook>` when it exists, and that directory is gitignored.
- Every action in every workflow is pinned to a commit SHA rather than a tag that can be moved under it, and a maintained catalogue of secret patterns now runs alongside the project's own rules.

### Fixed
- **Skipping the optional PIN during setup no longer locks the household out of Settings.** A parent PIN is optional at setup, but the settings gate asked for one regardless, and Settings is the only screen where a PIN can be set, so an instance created without one had no way back in. Settings now opens when no parent has a PIN, and is gated exactly as before as soon as any parent has one. Choosing a parent who has no PIN says so and points at Settings, Family Members, instead of showing a pad that nothing can complete, and the setup wizard now says what leaving the PIN blank means. ([#481](https://github.com/sandydargoport/prism/issues/481))

## [1.26.0] – 2026-09-12

### Added
- **An optional authentication wall.** Prism serves the family's calendar, messages, tasks and lists to anything that can reach it, which is exactly right for a screen on a kitchen wall and wrong for an instance reachable from anywhere else. A new setting in *Settings → Security*, **off by default**, requires a sign-in before anything is served. The screen in the kitchen is the exception: a parent marks that display trusted once, from the display itself, and it keeps working untouched while every other device has to sign in. Left off, nothing changes, guests can still read the board and still cannot alter it. Prism also gains a sign-in page, because signing in has always been a panel over a page that was already on screen, and with the wall on there is nothing underneath.
- **Event notes now show in the calendar, rendered rather than dumped.** An event carrying directions, a dial-in or a packing list showed nothing at all. Notes now appear in the detail view with their links live, and are sanitised on the way in, since the text arrives from whichever calendar you synced and is not yours.
- **Themes now control the status colours too.** Success, warning and error states were written as literal greens, ambers and reds in the markup, so a theme could not reach them: install a dark, muted palette and the "saved" ticks and "needs attention" banners stayed the same bright factory colours. Around 400 of those hardcoded colours across 66 files now read theme values instead. Two new colours, `success` and `warning`, join the palette a theme may set, alongside the error colour that already existed. Both are **optional**: a theme that says nothing about them keeps Prism's defaults, so every existing theme, including any installed from the gallery, looks exactly as it did. A theme that does set them is contrast-checked on them like any other pair. The per-widget icon colours on the dashboard are deliberately untouched, because those are a set of distinct hues doing a job that one shared colour cannot do.

### Changed
- **The month grid was rebuilt around one idea: a lane is a lane.** Multi-day events, all-day events and a day's own entries were three kinds of thing drawn three ways, and every fix to one broke the alignment of another. They are now the same object on the same grid. A run that crosses a week joins into a single pill instead of repeating its title on every row, says how much of it is left, and keeps a rounded cap where it genuinely ends while a chevron still means it continues. A blank lane is a slice like any other, so a day a span misses no longer reserves space it never uses, and a day's own events can use the room above a bar. There is a browser-level geometry suite in CI now, because every bug in this area has been a box in the wrong place, which no unit test can see.
- **Performance mode applies before the first paint.** A display with it switched on still drew one frame of the expensive version, backdrop blur on every card, and then removed it, on exactly the thin clients that can least afford the frame, and on every page load rather than the first.
- **A service worker install no longer downloads 3846 emoji.** The emoji set was 91% of what each display cached on install, 18MB fetched as 3846 separate requests, and every update made it do so again. A page uses a few dozen of them; they are now fetched as they are needed. On a thin client this is the difference you feel after an update.

### Fixed
- **Calendar reassignment now follows the event to Google.** Moving an event onto a Google calendar saved the change locally and made no API call at all, so it never appeared in Google and nothing said so. Moving one between two Google calendars now moves it there, and moving one away removes the upstream copy instead of leaving it to be re-imported as a duplicate. Moving a single occurrence of a repeating event is something Google refuses outright; that now says so plainly rather than reporting a general failure. Thanks to @sanko-oz.
- **An event with a long venue address can be edited again.** Calendars put the whole venue block in the location field (name, address, suite, parking notes), and anything past 255 characters made the event impossible to save, rejecting a field you had not touched and naming nothing. The form now also says which field it refused.
- **Clearing a description, a location or a reminder now sticks.** Emptying a field saved as empty and then came back on the next sync, because an absent value reads as "leave this alone" to the calendar it is being sent to.
- **The travel globe survives its mapping library's next major.** The upgrade would have left the globe drawing land shading with no water, no borders and no labels, with nothing logged and no error raised.
- **Photos from a synced source now filter by orientation.** Orientation was worked out only for photos uploaded by hand, so anything arriving from OneDrive or Immich was stored without one and the landscape/portrait filter on the Photos page returned nothing for it. That is the filter that keeps phone-shaped photos off a landscape display, and every photo joins the wallpaper rotation by default whatever its shape, so on a synced library there was no practical way to exclude them short of paging through the lot. Existing photos are fixed in place on update, using the dimensions already recorded, so nothing re-syncs and no photos are downloaded again.
- **A birthday no longer appears twice for the same person** when one source spells the name possessively and another does not.
- **A CalDAV event with a stray carriage return in its notes no longer breaks the whole sync.**

### Under the hood
- Now runs on Next 16. The service worker, the offline behaviour and the dashboard are unchanged; this is the framework underneath.
- A local deploy no longer overwrites the container's compiled modules with ones built for a different C library, which took an instance down for twenty-two minutes and reported it as an unrelated start-up error.
- Local database backups now expire on the date in their filename rather than a timestamp that other tools reset, which had the nightly check reporting a failure on backups that were working.
