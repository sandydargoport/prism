# Scenery

Landscape photographs bundled with Prism as wallpaper for new installs, shown until a household adds its own photos.

Each is a work of the US National Park Service, made by an employee as part of their official duties, and so in the public domain in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105; tagged PD-USGov-NPS on Wikimedia Commons). No permission or credit is required; the sources are listed so they can be checked.

Resized to 2560 × 1440 and converted to WebP.
